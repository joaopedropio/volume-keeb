GIF screensavers

8-bit GIFs and images → the knob's 428 × 142 screen ← Settings

1Import

Paste a link to a GIF or an image (PNG, JPEG, WebP; a Giphy/Tenor page works too), or drop / paste a file anywhere on the page. A still image makes a still screensaver.

2Pixels

Find the size of one "8-bit" pixel in the image, so each one becomes a single pixel again. Auto-detect usually gets it; check the grid lines sit on the pixel borders and compare Source and Result (hold the mouse on the image to flip).

3Screen

Each art pixel is shown as a square of screen pixels. Drag the frame (or click where it should go) to pick the part that ends up on the screen; scroll over it to change the scale. An image bigger than the screen can also be shrunk to fit (scales under 1×), losing some of its pixels.

4Preview

5Send to the knob

The knob keeps six GIF (or still image) screensavers in its flash (three with firmware before v0.7.0); they stay after a reboot or a firmware update. Pick the slot this one goes in.

6Download

The screen GIF is exactly what the panel shows. The art-pixel GIF is the same cut at one pixel per art pixel (the scale goes in its name).
